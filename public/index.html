01 The Driver

I'm a BSc (Hons) Computing student at Islington College (London Metropolitan University) in Kathmandu. I write production-grade code across the stack, build and evaluate machine learning models, and turn messy data into something people can actually act on.

My work swings between three worlds: web platforms (Django, Java servlets, plain honest JavaScript), applied ML (scikit-learn, TF-IDF, LangChain pipelines), and hardware (an ESP32 pentest device I soldered and broke more than once). In 2026 I became lead author on a peer-reviewed paper about AI fraud detection in Nepal's payment ecosystem.

Off the keyboard I watch too much F1 and reread too many comics. Both taught me the same thing: talent gets you to the grid, preparation wins the race.
